# BRIEF · קפה נחת (אתר דוגמה)

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Ravit asked for "an example site built with
scroll-craft, similar to nahat.check-it.co.il in terms of materials" and did not run the interview.
Content, photography and brand marks come from the live Nahat site (a MyMuse client build).
Everything below that is not quoted from that site is an authored decision, marked as such.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe (authored):** תחנת רכבת ישנה, עיתון בוקר, קלייה כהה, כיסא מתכת אדום בשמש.
   References: a Hebrew morning newspaper set in Frank Ruhl; an airline route map; the red folding chairs in Nahat's own terrace photo.
2. **Journey (from the site's own order):** the promise "עברנו את כל העולם בשביל הכוס הזאת", then the origins,
   then the story and direct trade, then the subscription, then the cafés, then Nahat for offices.
3. **Energy (authored):** calm title page; the origins are the loud, long stretch; story and subscription settle; cafés are warm; the close is quiet and firm.
4. **Feeling (authored):** curiosity, then wonder at the distance, trust, relief, belonging, invitation. The one moment is in the origins.
5. **Signature seed (authored):** the site's headline is a claim about distance, so the page should measure it.
6. **Range (authored):** editorial, one step away from premium-minimal: dense serif type, hard chapter cuts, real photography.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes. Chapters with hard cuts between grounds.
8. **Assets:** seven photos and two logo files from the Nahat site. No footage, no generation, no kie.ai spend.

## The peak

"When the last card passed, the five lines on the map added up to 42,000 km: more than once around the planet, for one cup."
It lives in פרק א׳, the longest act on the page (6.5 viewport-heights). The title page before it is the quietest screen.

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where you watch the coffee travel more than once around the world before it reaches your cup.

## Numbers used (all real or computed)

- 20+ years in coffee, specialty score 80+, subscription discounts 5% / 7% / 10%, free shipping over 150 ₪: quoted from the Nahat site.
- Distances: great-circle distance from each origin region to Tel Aviv, computed with the haversine formula and rounded to the nearest hundred
  (Gedeb 2,900 · Huila 11,800 · Daterra 10,400 · Sethuraman 4,700 · Antigua 12,200 · total about 42,000). Region points are approximate, so the page says "כ־".
